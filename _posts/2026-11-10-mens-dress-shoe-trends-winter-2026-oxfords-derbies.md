For a decade, the sneaker sat at the center of the men's wardrobe. This winter the leather shoe is taking that spot back. The runways, the trade fairs and the menswear editors all point the same way in 2026: proper Oxfords, versatile derbies and rich patina leathers are again the clearest sign of good taste. Here are the men's dress shoe trends for winter 2026, what they mean for your rotation, and the Goodyear-welted shoes worth investing in.

> **Key takeaways**
> - The fall/winter 2026 shows in Paris put leather shoes back in the lead role; sneakers now play a supporting part.
> - A smart winter rotation needs three roles: a black or dark brown Oxford, an everyday derby, and one pair with personality.
> - The derby is the most useful shoe a man can own in 2026, especially in brown with hand-finished patina.
> - Heritage collaborations like Barbour x Paraboot show where premium casual dress shoes are heading.
> - For winter, a Dainite rubber sole and a storm welt beat a thin leather sole, but no welted shoe is fully waterproof.

## 1. Fashion Week Moved Past Sneakers

Highsnobiety's report from Paris Fashion Week Men's Fall/Winter 2026 sums up the mood in its headline: fashion week finally moved past sneakers. AURALEE opened with suede apron derbies and soft leather loafers, Our Legacy showed almond-toed Oxfords, and Louis Vuitton sent out split-toe moccasins for a polished commute. Sneakers did not vanish, but the magazine argues that the "main character sneaker" is over and that loafers, brogues, boots and derbies are now the truest sign of modern good taste. For a man who already owns good sneakers, the message is simple: the next serious purchase should be leather.

## 2. The Three-Shoe Winter Rotation: Start With a Proper Oxford

You do not need a closet full of shoes to dress well this winter. Mezlan's fall/winter 2026 guide recommends a rotation built on three roles. First comes the foundation, a black or dark brown Oxford for suits, formal occasions and any moment that calls for traditional polish. Second is the everyday upgrade, a brown derby, loafer or monk strap that moves between tailored trousers, business casual and smart weekends. Third is the personality pair in suede, a rich color or a distinctive finish. The guide notes that the classic cap-toe Oxford now also comes in burgundy and mocha, which pair well with navy, charcoal and patterned tailoring, while black stays the choice for the most formal occasions.

Original article: [Mezlan](https://www.mezlan.com/blogs/news/the-mezlan-men-s-fall-shoe-guide-what-to-wear-for-fall-winter-2026)

## 3. Oxford vs Derby: Why the Derby Is the Most Useful Shoe of 2026

The difference between the two is in the lacing. An Oxford has closed lacing, with the eyelet tabs sewn under the vamp, which gives it a sleek, formal look. A derby has open lacing, with the tabs sewn on top, which makes it a little more relaxed and easier to fit. Mezlan calls the derby "one of the most useful shoes a man can own," because the same pair can work with a navy suit on Tuesday, tailored trousers and a knit polo on Friday, and dark denim with a sport coat on the weekend. The German shoemaker Sioux sees the same shift in its fall 2026 trend report: derby-style lace-ups are showing up more in collections and street style, and they are now worn with jeans, casual trousers and knitwear as a grown-up alternative to sneakers.

## 4. Patina Browns: Chocolate, Cognac, Rust and Mocha

Brown is no longer one flat color. Mezlan describes fall/winter 2026 as a season of chocolate, cognac, rust and mocha, built up through hand finishing and patina so the leather shows depth and changes slightly with the light. Sioux lists brown, cognac and Bordeaux among the key shoe colors of the season. For styling, the advice is to pair rich brown leather with charcoal flannel, navy wool, olive, camel, cream, dark denim or tonal browns. If you own only black dress shoes, a patina brown derby is the single most effective upgrade for winter tailoring.

Original article: [Mezlan](https://www.mezlan.com/blogs/news/the-mezlan-men-s-fall-shoe-guide-what-to-wear-for-fall-winter-2026)

## 5. Barbour x Paraboot: The Waxed-Cotton Chambord

One of the most talked-about new shoes this month is a heritage collaboration. Valet reports that Barbour and Paraboot are back together seven years after their first project, this time on Paraboot's Chambord, the apron-stitched Paris derby that has been a bestseller for more than 40 years. The upper mixes brown leather with Barbour's olive waxed cotton, the brand's classic tartan sits inside, and the shoe rides on Paraboot's Norwegian-welted rubber sole, complete with a co-branded tassel. Valet suggests wearing it with grey flannels, dark denim or heavy cords. It is a good example of the premium casual dress shoe: rugged enough for a wet city, refined enough for a sport coat.

## 7. The Best Dress Shoes for Winter: Dainite Soles and Storm Welts

Salt, rain and slush are hard on thin leather soles, and the shoe enthusiasts on Reddit's r/goodyearwelt have a clear answer for winter. Their favorite winter dress shoes combine a Dainite (studded rubber) sole with a storm welt and hard-wearing scotch grain leather; one member called Crockett & Jones Dainite shoes "essentially impervious to weather." Another common tip is to have a cobbler add a thin rubber topy to leather soles. The British retailer A Fine Pair of Shoes adds an important caveat in its 2026 wet-weather guide: Dainite improves grip and durability, but it does not make a shoe waterproof. A Goodyear-welted shoe is water-resistant, not waterproof, so keep the uppers conditioned and polished all winter.

## FAQ

### What are the men's dress shoe trends for winter 2026?

The main trends are leather shoes replacing sneakers as the lead shoe, classic Oxfords and versatile derbies, hand-finished patina browns such as chocolate, cognac, rust and mocha, suede and velvet textures, and rugged rubber-soled dress shoes for bad weather.

### What is the difference between an Oxford and a derby?

An Oxford has closed lacing, with the eyelet tabs sewn under the front of the shoe, so it looks sleeker and more formal. A derby has open lacing, with the tabs sewn on top, so it is more relaxed, easier to fit and easier to wear with both suits and denim.

### Can you wear dress shoes with a rubber sole in winter?

Yes. A Dainite or similar rubber sole gives better grip on wet pavement and wears longer than leather. It looks slightly less formal, so many men keep a leather-soled Oxford for dry days and wear a rubber-soled derby for commuting. Rubber soles add grip, but they do not make a shoe waterproof.

### Which dress shoe should a man buy first?

Most style guides start with a black or dark brown Oxford for suits and formal occasions. The second pair is usually a brown derby or loafer for business casual and weekends.
