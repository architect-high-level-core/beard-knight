The clocks have gone back, the sidewalks are wet, and it is officially boot season. The big men's boot trends for fall 2026 are clear: the Chelsea boot is back at the center, toes are getting a little sharper, suede is getting softer and richer, and rugged lug soles are moving from the trail into the city.

Here is what the runways and the US style press are saying right now, and which premium boots are worth the investment.

## 1. The Chelsea Boot Is Having a Moment Again

At Milan Men's Fashion Week for Fall 2026, Chelsea boots showed up everywhere. Paul Smith paired polished leather versions with flannel business suits, while Brunello Cucinelli sent out velvety suede and black leather Chelsea boots worn under straight-leg jeans. Even Saul Nash showed a Chelsea in his collaboration with Manolo Blahnik. The message: one elastic-sided boot that works with tailoring and denim alike.

Original article: [WWD / Footwear News](https://wwd.com/footwear-news/shoe-trends/fall-2026-men-shoes-trends-milan-fashion-week-1238494269/)

## 2. Toes Are Getting Pointier

The round, chunky toe is not the only option anymore. In its big autumn/winter 2026 trend guide, GQ's style team says boots are getting "pointier", and the Milan runways backed that up with slightly sharper Chelsea toes at Paul Smith. A sleeker toe makes a boot look more elegant under suit trousers and slimmer straight-leg jeans. If you only own one pair of boots, a gently tapered (not winklepicker) toe is the most versatile choice.

Original article: [GQ](https://www.gq-magazine.co.uk/article/autumn-winter-2026-menswear-trends)

## 3. Lugged, Low-Profile Hikers for the City

GQ's fall 2026 footwear guide highlights a wave of low-top, weather-ready hikers with chunky lug soles, D-ring eyelets and mixes of leather and rubber. American heritage makers like Allen Edmonds and G.H. Bass give the shape a more polished, minimal spin in autumnal olive suede or glossy black leather. The idea: outdoor grip and attitude, but clean enough for a good bar on a Friday night.

Original article: [GQ](https://www.gq.com/story/boots-shoes-sneakers-fall-2026)

## 4. Weather-Ready Chelsea Boots: Rubber Soles Win

Esquire's new guide to weatherproof Chelsea boots (October 19, 2026) gives a simple rule for rainy commutes: choose rubber soles, not leather. Leather soles are porous, soak up water and wear out fast on wet concrete, and winter road salt is especially hard on them. The second rule is care: you do not need to shine weekly, but keep boots clean and add a little polish, mink oil or a beeswax-based seal now and then. Esquire's splurge pick is the R.M. Williams Comfort Craftsman.

Original article: [Esquire](https://www.esquire.com/style/a74091357/best-waterproof-chelsea-boots/)

## 5. How to Buy Suede Boots That Last

Suede is the texture of the season, but quality varies a lot. FashionBeans quotes a simple tip from footwear expert Godding: short-pile suede is a sign of quality and wears better, while long-pile suede gets scruffy quickly. Treated or "repello" suede adds real water resistance, so a suede Chelsea can handle more than a dry afternoon. Spray new suede with a protector before the first wear and brush it after rain once it is dry.

Original article: [FashionBeans](https://www.fashionbeans.com/article/best-chelsea-boots-men)

## How to Wear Chelsea Boots This Fall

- **With jeans:** straight-leg denim that falls cleanly over the shaft, like the Cucinelli runway look. Skip skinny jeans tucked in.
- **With tailoring:** polished black or dark brown leather with flannel trousers and a slightly sharper toe.
- **Smart casual:** dark brown suede with chinos or cords, a cashmere crewneck and a camel overcoat.
- **Color:** dark brown and black are the safest first pair; sand or cedar suede is the stylish second pair.

## FAQ

### What are the biggest men's boot trends for fall 2026?
Chelsea boots with slightly pointier toes, velvety suede in dark brown and sand, and low-profile hikers with chunky lug soles are the key men's boot trends for fall 2026.

### Are Chelsea boots still in style in 2026?
Yes. Chelsea boots were one of the standout styles at Milan Men's Fashion Week for Fall 2026, worn both with flannel suits and with straight-leg jeans.

### Can you wear suede boots in the rain?
Treated or water-repellent suede handles rain much better than untreated suede. Choose a rubber sole, spray with a protector before first wear, and brush the suede once it has dried.

### What is the best sole for Chelsea boots in winter?
A rubber sole. It grips better on wet sidewalks and does not soak up water or suffer from road salt the way a leather sole does.
