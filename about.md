<div class="page-body" markdown="1">

**Beard Knight** is a blog about men's style and grooming – *everything you need to know as a man*. It is run by **Arnold Kahr**.

## What the blog covers

- **Beard and grooming guides:** oil, balm, shaving, hair, skin care and seasonal routines.
- **Clothes and accessories:** coats, boots, gloves, watches and how to wear them.
- **Plain English:** practical advice with sources linked, so you can check them yourself.
- **Read-aloud audio:** English posts have an audio version – press **▶ Listen** to hear the article.

## How the posts are made

Posts are researched with AI assistance from the linked sources, then curated and published by Arnold Kahr. No invented biography is claimed beyond what is stated on this page.
